ABOUT

Bringing order
to business data.

Every record matters. Finding the right information should never be complicated.

DataSpace is a business record management system designed to help businesses manage information in a more organized and efficient way. It provides a structured environment for adding, managing, sorting, and searching records.

By applying fundamental data structures and algorithms, the system aims to reduce the time spent locating information and improve the overall organization of business records.

HOW IT WORKS

From scattered records
to organized data.

DataSpace follows a straightforward process that helps users manage their information from the moment a record is entered until the information is retrieved.

STEP 01 Input Add business records
STEP 02 Store Save information in a list
STEP 03 Sort Arrange records using Insertion Sort
STEP 04 Search Find and retrieve records
